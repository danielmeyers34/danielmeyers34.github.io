ZeroDegen Research Programme

● Live

A falsification-first research stack: every strategy idea is pre-registered, replayed on six years of data with a random-entry placebo and a drift-preserving bootstrap null, a positive control through the same pipeline, and a held-out window. ~53 verdicts in a public-style ledger so nothing gets re-chased; a market feature library that measures what state predicts (move size: yes; direction: no). "Nothing here" is an allowed answer, and usually the honest one.

PythonNumPyStatisticsPre-registration
